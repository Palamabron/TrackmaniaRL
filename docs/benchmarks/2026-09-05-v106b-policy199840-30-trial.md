## Verdict

This checkpoint does **not** yet prove reliable sub-37 performance.

* It finished 29 of 30 trials.
* Its completed-lap mean was **37.359 s**, median **36.800 s** and best **36.560 s**.
* The configured benchmark gate failed because it required 30/30 finishes. The separate project
  objective of completed-lap mean below 37 s also failed.
* No trial was below 36 s.
* The typical policy is already fast enough for a sub-37 mean: the 27 completed laps below 38 s
  averaged **36.807 s** with population standard deviation **0.137 s**. The aggregate was lost to
  two slow finishes (47.080 s and 42.550 s) and one DNF at 75.369% progress.

The immediate problem is therefore **tail robustness and recovery**, not nominal pace. Architecture
and line-quality work remains necessary for a repeatable best below 36 s.

This benchmark was not screen-recorded. It is diagnostic evidence, not release-proof video.

## What failed

### Rare failures dominate the mean

The 27 ordinary finishes have mean 36.807 s. Replacing both slow finishes and the DNF with an
ordinary lap would put the 30-trial mean near 36.81 s. Conversely, even one 47 s lap is enough to
destroy the mean target. More training that only improves the centre of the distribution is unlikely
to solve this.

The two slow completed laps began diverging from the normal progress trace around 35-40% and 80-85%.
The DNF stopped at 75.369%. These locations make the 35-45% and 75-100% sectors the highest-value
targets for video, lateral-clearance and recovery diagnostics.

### Runtime and telemetry are possible contributors

Trials 3 and 4 recorded 269 and 249 skipped telemetry frames, respectively. Together they account
for 518 of the benchmark's 664 skipped frames. This is a correlation, not proof: their recorded race
step still normally remained at 50 ms, so drained/stale packets need not have caused the bad line.

Trial 19 did contain a real timing disturbance around 70-75% progress: a race step exceeded one
second and the action was consequently held. Architecture alone cannot repair an operating-system,
capture, game or transport stall. The next benchmark must record the Trackmania window and preserve
the timing trace so visual cause and telemetry can be aligned.

### The current collision metric must not drive reward tuning

The collision flag is phase-like in this run: trials 0-18 are false, trial 19 is true and every trial
20-29 remains true despite ordinary 36.56-37.07 s finishes. It is a rumble/contact proxy, not a count
of physical crashes. It cannot distinguish an efficient light touch from long wall grinding or a
terminal crash. Do not infer that the policy has exactly two optimal collisions and do not build a
new reward around this bit alone.

A useful future contact signal should measure contact duration, entry speed, speed/progress loss and
recovery time. That permits cheap touches while strongly penalising contact that actually costs time.

## Architecture and feature audit

The current system is a 20 Hz dueling IQN policy with `BoundaryGraphFeaturePipelineV2`, a
`TrackGnnSimbaEncoderV2`, four SimbaV2 residual blocks, an identity temporal core and 78 discrete
actions.

### Skip connections

The SimbaV2 backbone already has four residual/LERP blocks. Adding generic skip connections to that
backbone duplicates an existing mechanism and does not address the measured failure. Residual paths
are still useful when adding new context, spatial or temporal modules because they can preserve the
current checkpoint at initialization.

### Missing high-value state

The V2 observation contains velocity, yaw rate, acceleration, relative heading, input echoes and 44
curvature samples. It does not explicitly expose:

* signed lateral offset from the intended path;
* left and right boundary clearance / road margin;
* absolute corner identity beyond scalar progress;
* rear-wheel slip, RPM and richer adherence/contact state;
* time since contact or accumulated speed loss;
* a speed-adaptive preview and target braking demand.

These are more direct explanations for wall proximity and recovery than pixels.

The fixed 44 x 2.5 m lookahead is about 110 m. At 80 m/s it represents only about 1.4 seconds. A
time-indexed, multi-scale preview can expose both the immediate racing line and braking requirements
several seconds ahead. Useful derived inputs include signed curvature, curvature change,
`speed^2 * abs(curvature)` and required deceleration to a future speed target.

### Geometry encoder

`TrackNeighborGraph` uses two symmetric neighbour-message layers and a global mean pool. It has no
explicit positional encoding and predecessor/successor directions are not distinguished. The ordered
44-curvature vector still survives through the separate physics branch, so the complete policy is not
fully order-blind, but the graph path discards useful order and current-to-future distance information.

For this fixed one-map problem, an ordered dilated Conv1D residual adapter is a lower-risk first test
than replacing the model with a large transformer. If it saturates, add a small car-conditioned global
attention block with explicit arc-length/time positional encoding. Do not use attention over an
unordered set of points.

The existing directional, arc-length and transformer encoders are V1-oriented: pairing them directly
with V2 would mask or mis-scale some V2 yaw, acceleration, heading, input and skid fields. A test must
use a V2-specific encoder or adapter. The earlier arc-length scalar experiment also failed its offline
imitation gates, so repeating that exact variant is not justified.

### Temporal state

The identity temporal core and sequence length 1 cannot retain a developing slide, a recent contact or
an action/timing disturbance beyond the hand-derived one-step quantities. Start with a cheap 4-8 frame
stack (0.2-0.4 s). If it helps, test a residual, zero-gated GRU with sequence length about 16 and burn-in
about 8. A plain randomly initialized GRU would destroy the warm-start policy and should not be bundled
with the spatial change.

### Vision

Raw vision is not the next experiment. On a fixed known map, exact telemetry and vector geometry are
cheaper, lower-latency and easier to diagnose. Vision becomes justified only if synchronized recordings
show that the failures depend on surface, kerb or obstacle semantics absent from the structured state.

## Recommended experiment ladder

Change one family at a time. Do not commit several days to a bundled architecture before a short gate
shows that it improves the tail.

### E0: controlled policy screen, no training

1. Record the Trackmania window and retain the benchmark JSON/timing trace.
2. Re-run the exact champion as a baseline under an otherwise idle system.
3. Screen action-selection `switch_q_margin` values `0.005`, `0.01` and `0.02` over 10 trials each.
   The current Q margins are often almost tied and the policy changes action much more frequently than
   the human reference. A small hysteresis may remove steering chatter cheaply. Do not start with a
   two-step minimum hold; it can make an incorrect high-speed action persist longer.
4. Confirm only the winner over 30 recorded trials.

Screen gate: 10/10 finishes, median below 37 s, no lap above 38 s and no telemetry/controller error.

Confirmation gate: 30/30 finishes, completed-lap mean below 37 s, median no worse than the 36.800 s
baseline, p95 below 37.5 s and no telemetry/controller errors. `best < 36 s` remains a secondary gate.

### E1: V3 residual context features

Preserve the trained V2 encoder, Simba backbone and IQN head. Add a context MLP whose last layer and
learned scale are initialized to zero, then add its 192-dimensional correction to the existing physics
latent. At step zero the policy is exactly the champion; learning can only introduce corrections.

First feature set:

* signed lateral offset and normalized offset;
* left/right clearance and minimum time-to-boundary;
* half-width / local width;
* Fourier or learned progress embedding;
* speed-adaptive curvature/braking summaries at approximately 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 6 seconds.

Use a fresh replay buffer and model-only warm start. Keep reward, learning rate and all other model
families fixed. Run three short seeds (250k-500k transitions is sufficient for selection), then give
only a winner the multi-day budget.

### E2: ordered residual spatial adapter

Add a zero-initialized ordered Conv1D branch over the boundary/curvature sequence, with directional
or dilated receptive fields such as 1/2/4/8. Add its output to the existing trained track latent. If
the convolution wins but still has identifiable distant-corner failures, compare it with a small
position-aware attention block. This isolates spatial order from the E1 state change.

### E3: short memory

Compare the E1/E2 winner with a short frame stack. Only if the stack helps, add the zero-gated residual
GRU and sequence replay with burn-in. Do not change spatial encoder and temporal core in the same run.

### E4: line and control improvements for best below 36 s

The present best is 36.560 s, so stability alone cannot satisfy this gate. The next likely gains are:

* a racing-line and pace profile derived from several clean human laps materially faster than the
  current checkpoint, ideally `<= 36.2 s` and preferably `< 36 s`;
* later braking/apex/exit-speed targets built from those trajectories;
* finer or hybrid/continuous steering, or at least an action-delta constraint, after measuring where
  the 13 steering levels lose time.

New ordinary 36.5-37.0 s demonstrations add little to the existing pace. Record clean 35.x/low-36.x
laps and deliberate recoveries in separate sets. The immediate safe use is to derive a static median
line and speed profile. Direct V2 behaviour cloning/DQfD must wait until stored control-echo fields are
shifted causally; same-frame controls otherwise leak the label.

## Recording protocol for the next benchmark

Before issuing the benchmark command:

1. Start window-only recording of Trackmania with hardware encoding when available.
2. Show or record a wall-clock cue at the start so video can be aligned with `evaluation.json`.
3. Keep the machine otherwise idle and do not place an overlay over the road.
4. Preserve the unedited full run for diagnosis; a short best-lap clip can be exported later for the
   README.
5. Record which trial contains every visible wall contact, spin, reset, menu interruption or game
   hitch. Compare these timestamps with skipped frames and decision-interval errors.

Recording itself can add load. If timing errors appear only with recording, reduce capture overhead or
use hardware encoding before drawing a model conclusion.

## Research basis

These sources motivate experiments; none guarantees a lap-time gain in this codebase.

* [TMRL](https://github.com/trackmania-rl/tmrl) uses short observation/action histories at 20 Hz and
  reports that history permits the agent to infer higher-order dynamics needed for braking, apex and
  acceleration decisions.
* [R2D2](https://willdabney.com/publication/r2d2/) motivates recurrent replay with burn-in rather than
  treating a recurrent state as automatically valid after random replay sampling.
* [GT Sophy](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04357-7) used rich vehicle
  state, speed-adaptive course preview and severity-sensitive wall-contact objectives.
* [Formula RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.11106) reports benefits from future curvature and discusses
  temporal and control-resolution trade-offs in autonomous racing.
* [LaneGCN](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2020/papers_ECCV/papers/123470528.pdf),
  [DeepGCN](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Li_DeepGCNs_Can_GCNs_Go_As_Deep_As_CNNs_ICCV_2019_paper.html)
  and [GraphGPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/5d4834a159f1547b267a05a4e2b7cf5e-Paper-Conference.pdf)
  motivate directional/dilated local structure, residual graph processing and position-aware global
  attention.
* [VectorNet](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Gao_VectorNet_Encoding_HD_Maps_and_Agent_Dynamics_From_Vectorized_Representation_CVPR_2020_paper.html)
  supports testing compact vector-map representations before raster vision.

## Decision

Do not launch another undifferentiated multi-day training run. First reproduce the tail with recorded
10-trial screens, select a conservative action margin, then build E1 as the first learning experiment.
Promote a model only after a recorded 30-trial confirmation passes the mean, finish-rate, p95 and
health gates above.
