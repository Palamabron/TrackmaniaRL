Data notes and figure credits

Development charts

The development charts include all 18, 10 and 4 evaluation batches from the three training histories. Means include finished attempts only. When no attempt finishes, the lap-time mean is undefined. The dashed connection bridges that gap without inventing a measurement. The finish-count panel preserves every batch.

The finish-time reward run continues the initial model’s checkpoint with a fresh logged transition counter. These are different training stages with different histories and batch sizes. They are not a controlled comparison of algorithms. Download chart data.

Thirty-attempt confirmation

All 30 attempts finished. The mean was 36.976667 s, the median 36.835 s, the best 36.560 s and the slowest 41.370 s. Every attempt, including the slowest one, is included. The series recorded 458 skipped telemetry frames. Download all trial measurements.

The reference states used by the action filter were fixed before the benchmark. The benchmark attempts were not added to them. The result describes the tested controller on one map.

Recordings

The benchmark clip is attempt 29 from the complete confirmation. Its telemetry time is 36.560 s and the game overlay reads 36.568 s. The original clip is 1280×720 at 20 fps.

The neural-flow film shows attempt 2 from a separate five-attempt series. Those five times were 40.820, 36.650, 36.750, 36.760 and 36.860 s. Their mean was 37.568 s. The fastest lap was selected for the film. Its overlay reads 36.658 s. This series illustrates the network in action and does not replace the thirty-attempt confirmation.

The neural-flow film is the original 1920×1080 video at 30 fps. Both clips use MP4 playback and retain their source resolution. No additional training took place during capture.
