W&B and Git

The three requested projects were read through the W&B API. Their metadata establishes 681 logging records. Older projects split trainer and worker into separate records. Counts also include evaluations, smoke tests and restarts.

The older 206 records have no recorded commit. Of the 475 records in my-trackmania-agent, 469 have a HEAD hash, but a hash does not establish that the working tree was clean. Several hashes refer to historical branches or rewritten history and are not ancestors of the current remote main. They are retained below as provenance, not linked as though they were the current public implementation.

Examples: V26 records 65fa3afc; related code exists in current history at 3f583994. V27 records 3293970b; related current code is 1e1c4c25. September runs record e4de10e4; related current code is 166e12f4. These are contextual correspondences, not claims of byte-for-byte identity.

Local runtime investigations

The V97 and V99 reports are local, ignored research files, not files in the remote-main snapshot. The V97 report (experiments/v97-tomr6uq1-forensics.md) records that 85% of random actions preserved the policy's drive mode, which was brake-biased, and the first 414 episodes barely moved. The related W&B run is tomr6uq1. The causal diagnosis comes from the local code/log investigation, not from the dashboard name.

The V99 report (experiments/v99-8xi45jc3-forensics.md) records mean replay-sampling time falling from 106.3 to 22.2 ms in its local benchmark after introducing the columnar fast path. This is a replay microbenchmark, not a 4.79× improvement in lap time or end-to-end training. Related W&B run: 8xi45jc3.

Local notes and author context

The MIQN account is based on the local reports/miqn_experiments_report.md, whose later full-history analysis corrects some earlier sampled-history claims. The article uses it for qualitative development context, not a ranking based on its early summaries.

The V105 reward example is recorded in the local SUB37.md experiment journal and independently verified against full W&B history: V105A episode 875 took 37.18 s for a return of 346.2128; episode 884 took 39.39 s for a return of 347.2299. The V105B five-attempt mean and 47/50 aggregate refer to development evaluations.

The Campaign1 numbers come from local docs/campaign1-architecture-decision.md and research notes. These were not committed in the article's remote-main snapshot and do not appear in the three inspected W&B project inventories, which end on September 8. They are exploratory evidence on another map, not a comparison against V108. The author clarified that complete track boundaries recordable through driving exist only on a minority of maps in their experience; this is the practical motivation for vision, not a measured census of maps.
